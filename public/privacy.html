Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 July 2026

PokeStrikers is a small fan-run community site for sharing Pokémon TCG Live promo codes. This page explains exactly what we store, why, and who can see it. We have tried to keep it plain rather than lawyerly.

What we collect

When you create an account we store:

  • Your username. Chosen by you. It is shown publicly next to anything you post.
  • Your password, hashed. Stored as a PBKDF2-SHA256 hash with a random salt. We never store, log, or have any way to read your actual password.
  • The date you joined.

As you use the site we also store:

  • Codes you upload, and codes you claim, each with a timestamp.
  • Which card sets you follow, so we can notify you about them.
  • Notifications generated for you, and whether you have read them.
  • Messages you post in the public chat.
  • How many codes you have claimed in the current 24-hour window, and whether you have unlocked the bonus code.

What we do not collect

  • No email address. Signing up asks only for a username and a password.
  • No real name, address, phone number, or date of birth.
  • No payment details. Nothing on this site is for sale.
  • No advertising or analytics trackers of our own.

The camera scanner

The code scanner on the upload page runs entirely inside your browser. Barcode and text recognition happen on your device, and no photo, video frame, or camera data is ever sent to us or to anyone else. Only the text of the code you choose to submit is uploaded. Your browser will ask permission before the camera turns on, and you can revoke it at any time.

How you stay logged in

We do not use tracking cookies. When you log in we issue a signed token that is stored in your browser's local storage and sent back to us with each request. It expires after 30 days. Clearing your browser data logs you out but does not delete your account.

What other people can see

Please assume the following are public:

  • Your username.
  • Anything you post in the community chat, which is readable by anyone, including people who are not logged in and search engines.
  • That a code was uploaded or claimed. The code-status checker will show the username of whoever claimed a given code.

The site administrator can additionally see the full list of accounts, join dates, and upload and claim activity.

Other services we rely on

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and its database. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical information such as your IP address and request logs in order to serve pages and protect against abuse.
  • YouTube supplies the embedded video used to unlock a bonus code. When that video loads, YouTube and Google may set their own cookies and collect data under their own privacy policy, which we do not control.
  • jsDelivr serves the text-recognition library used by the scanner. Loading it reveals your IP address to that CDN.

How long we keep things

Account details are kept until the account is removed. Promo codes remain in the database, and once a code has been claimed or has expired we permanently record the code itself so it cannot be uploaded again; that record contains only the code, not who used it. Chat messages are kept until deleted.

Your choices

You can stop using the site at any time, and unfollow any set from the Notifications page.

To delete your account, go to your profile page and use "Delete your account". You will be asked for your password to confirm. This erases your account, your chat messages, your followed sets and your notifications immediately and permanently.

Two things deliberately survive that deletion. Codes you uploaded stay in the vault so other trainers can still claim them, but they are unlinked from you, as are codes you claimed — nothing left behind identifies you. And the record that a particular code has already been used is kept, because it is what stops a spent code being uploaded again; that record contains only the code itself and has never been linked to any account.

If you cannot log in, email us using the details below and we will remove the account for you. Because we do not collect an email address, we may need another way to confirm the account is yours.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we ask that under-13s do not create an account. If you believe a child has created one, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page.
